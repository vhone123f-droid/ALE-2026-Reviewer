ALE 2026 OFFICIAL-TOS FALLBACK SUMMARY

Source of authority:
Professional Regulatory Board of Agriculture Resolution No. 04, Series of 2024,
"Adoption and Issuance of the Enhanced Tables of Specification for the Subjects
in the Licensure Examination for Agriculturists." It applies beginning with the
November 2024 Agriculturists Licensure Examination and onward.

The six examination subjects are weighted approximately equally:
1. Crop Science — 16.7%
2. Soil Science — 16.7%
3. Crop Protection — 16.7%
4. Animal Science — 16.67%
5. Agricultural Economics and Marketing — 16.7%
6. Agricultural Extension and Communication — 16.7%

The enhanced TOS uses Revised Bloom's Taxonomy and includes substantial
Applying and Analyzing items, so the reviewer should emphasize scenarios,
calculations, diagnosis, interpretation, and decisions, not only recall.

Examples explicitly visible in the official TOS include:
- Crop Protection: plant pathogens, disease cycle and epidemiology, arthropod
  and vertebrate pests, natural enemies, pest management, biotechnology,
  GM/Bt technology and resistance management, SMART agriculture, artificial
  intelligence in crop protection, and urban-farming pest management.
- Soil Science: soil genesis and development, mineral vs organic soils,
  soil profile, and management/interpretation of soil properties.
- Animal Science: animals and their environment and economic utility plus
  production, nutrition, breeding/reproduction, health, management and related
  animal-agriculture competencies.
- Agricultural Economics and Marketing: production functions, costs,
  diminishing returns, demand, supply, elasticity, price/output determination,
  consumer behavior, farm/business analysis and agricultural marketing.
- Agricultural Extension and Communication: Philippine extension context,
  extension approaches and methods, roles of extension workers, technology
  attributes, communication processes/models/strategies, diffusion/adoption,
  program planning and M&E, and adult learning/andragogy.

This fallback is only used if an official URL cannot be downloaded during a
scheduled workflow. The automation should prefer the live official documents.
